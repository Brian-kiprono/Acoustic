ARCHITECTURE & CRAFT

About & Technical Notes

Creative Concept

ACOUSTIC treats letterforms as physical, resonant membranes suspended in dark space. Words respond to voice, music, and touch through wave propagation, surface tension, and Fresnel edge luminescence.

Web Audio API & Spectral Analysis

A single AudioContext and AnalyserNode (FFT 2048) sample live microphone streams or uploaded audio tracks strictly on user action. Frequency bins are mapped into bass, mid, and treble bands alongside overall amplitude and spectral distribution.

GLSL Shaders & Frequency Coupling

Vertex displacement combines 3D Simplex noise, overlapping waves, and spectral uniforms directly on the GPU, recomputing surface normals via finite differences so wave crests catch crisp specular highlights without CPU buffer uploads.

Procedural Geometry & Interaction

Individual beveled glyphs are positioned with custom tracking and alignment, merged, and adaptively subdivided into uniform triangles so cursor push/pull forces and radial click ripples deform smoothly at 60 FPS.
